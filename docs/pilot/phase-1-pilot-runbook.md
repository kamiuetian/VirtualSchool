# Phase 1 Pilot Runbook

## Pilot Size

50-300 school-owned Android tablets across 2-5 classes.

## Pilot Duration

4-8 weeks after enrollment and staff training.

## Required Scenarios

- Daily Class Mode.
- Homework Mode.
- Break Mode.
- App and web allowlists.
- Secure browser default-deny.
- Remote lock.
- Lost-device drill.
- App rollout and removal.
- Offline policy behavior.
- Policy rollback.
- Device inventory and deployment exports.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Devices enroll reliably.
- Students access only approved apps and websites during managed modes.
- Mode switching follows schedules and overrides.
- Admins can identify offline, non-compliant, and failed-deployment devices.
- Privileged actions are authenticated, authorized, and audited.
- Last-known policy remains enforced during reasonable connectivity interruptions.
